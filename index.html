One week notice at work: when 7 days is enough

Seven days is the shortest notice most contracts allow. Here is when it is legal, when it costs you money, and how to hand over properly in five working days.

Updated September 2026  ·  Reading time about 4 minutes  ·  Topic: Employment and notice periods

Day 1 is the day after you hand in the letter. Weekends count unless your contract says working days.

On this page

    A one week notice is the shortest resignation period most people can give without breaking something. Sometimes it is exactly what the contract asks for. Sometimes it burns a reference you will want in three years. The difference comes down to what your contract says, what your local law sets as a floor, and how much handover work only you can do.

    What a one week notice actually means

    Notice starts the day after you hand it in, unless your contract says otherwise. Hand in a resignation on a Tuesday and a 7 day notice usually runs to the end of the following Tuesday. Calendar days, not working days, unless the contract spells out working days.

    Two things trip people up here. The first is that weekends and public holidays still count in a calendar day notice, so a week over Christmas can mean two working days of handover. The second is that the notice period and your last paid day are not always the same thing, especially if you have unused leave.

    Write the end date in the letter. Do not write "one week from today" and leave it to interpretation. Write the exact final working day. Every payroll dispute I have seen over short notice started with a vague resignation letter.

    Is one week enough under the law

    In most countries the statutory minimum for a short tenure employee is very short, and one week clears it. A few reference points:

    Statutory minimum notice from employee to employer, common examples
    Country Typical statutory floor What contracts usually ask for
    United Kingdom 1 week after 1 month of service 1 month, or 3 months for senior roles
    United States None. At-will in 49 states 2 weeks by custom, not law
    India Set by contract and state shops acts 30 to 90 days
    Germany 4 weeks to the 15th or month end Same, longer during probation exceptions

    The statutory floor is the fallback. Your signed contract almost always asks for more, and that is the number that matters in a dispute. If your contract says one month, giving a week is a breach, even though a week clears the legal minimum.

    During probation the rules loosen. Many contracts drop notice to 7 days or even 24 hours in the first few months, which is why the number shows up so often alongside 90 day probation periods.

    When a week is fine

    • You are still inside probation and the contract sets a 7 day period.
    • You are a contractor with a short notice clause, or on a rolling weekly engagement.
    • You are hourly, easily covered, and the rota is published a week ahead.
    • The employer has already made the role redundant and asked you to go early.
    • Your work is documented, and nothing lives only in your head or your inbox.

    When a week costs you money

    Three real costs, in order of how often they bite.

    Clawback clauses

    Signing bonuses, relocation payments, visa fees and training costs often come with a repayment clause tied to short notice or early exit. Leaving badly can turn a bonus into an invoice.

    Garden leave and final pay

    If you leave without giving the contractual period, some employers deduct the shortfall from your final pay. Whether that is enforceable depends on your country and whether the contract stated it in advance.

    The reference

    Most written references are neutral now, but the informal call still happens. A manager left holding an unfinished handover remembers it for years.

    How to write a one week resignation

    Keep it to five lines. Date, role, final working day, a short handover offer, a thank you. No reasons, no complaints, no feedback. Save all of that for the exit conversation where it cannot be forwarded.

    Please accept this as notice of my resignation from the Accounts Assistant role. My final working day will be Friday 24 October. I will finish the October reconciliations and write a handover note for the open supplier queries before I leave.

    Send it by email so the timestamp is fixed, then follow with a paper copy if HR asks. The timestamp is what proves the notice start date if pay is later disputed.

    Making seven days useful

    Seven days is enough time to hand over properly if you spend it on the right things. Day 1 and 2, write the handover document, no meetings. Day 3 and 4, walk a named person through the live work and give them the passwords through the proper tool. Day 5, transfer ownership of files, shared drives, recurring meetings and any vendor account in your name. Day 6, sit with your manager and go through anything still open. Day 7, clear personal files and confirm your final pay details.

    Do not spend the last afternoon on a long goodbye email. Spend it making sure nobody has to message you after you leave.

    Other places a 7 day clock appears

    The one week count shows up well beyond resignations. Change of mind rights on doorstep and distance sales run 7 or 14 days in many jurisdictions. Some airlines hold a fare for 7 days. Short term rentals often require 7 days notice to end a weekly tenancy. Statutory sick pay in several systems starts after a 7 day self certification window.

    Longer versions of the same rule show up as two week notice periods, 30 day money back windows and 60 day return policies. Same structure, different length, and the counting rule is identical in all of them: start counting from the day after the trigger event.

    Common questions

    Does notice start on the day I hand in the letter?

    Usually the day after. Unless your contract states the notice runs from the date of the letter, count the following day as day 1 and the seventh day after that as your last day.

    Can my employer refuse a one week notice?

    They cannot force you to keep working, but if the contract sets a longer period, leaving early can be a breach of contract. That can affect final pay and any bonus repayment clause.

    Do weekends count in a seven day notice?

    Yes, unless the contract says working days. That is why a calendar week over a public holiday can leave you with only two or three working days of handover.

    Is one week notice normal during probation?

    It is common. Many contracts set a short notice period during the first one to three months and increase it once probation is confirmed.
